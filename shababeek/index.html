Privacy notice

سياسة الخصوصية

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Only what you type into our booking, catering and careers forms: your name, phone number and the details of your request.

How we use it

Only to answer your booking, enquiry or application. We don't sell it and we don't use it for marketing.

Sent through WhatsApp

These forms don't send anything to a server. When you submit, we prepare a WhatsApp message and you decide whether to send it. Once sent, it is handled under WhatsApp's own terms.

Cookies and tracking

We use no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The only thing we save is your language choice, stored in your own browser, and you can clear it at any time. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the menu QR code library from cdnjs, which see your IP address as any web request does.

Deleting your details

To ask us to delete messages you've sent, call +971 6 554 0444 or message us on WhatsApp at +971 50 116 0022.
